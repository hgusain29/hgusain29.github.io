Once you have downloaded **tinypdf**, follow these steps to set it up:

2. **Extract the Files**:

**tinypdf** runs smoothly on most modern systems. Here are the basic requirements:

## 📄 How to Create a PDF
Creating a PDF with **tinypdf** is straightforward. Follow these simple steps:

1. **Open the Application**: Launch **tinypdf** if it’s not already open.

2. **Select a Template**: Choose a template from the options provided. You can select a blank document or an invoice template.

3. **Fill in Your Details**: Add text, images, or any other content you want in your PDF. The user-friendly interface allows you to customize your document easily.

4. **Save Your PDF**: Once you are satisfied with your document, click on the "Save" button. Choose a location on your computer and give your file a name.

5. **Done!**: Your PDF is ready to use. You can share it or print it as needed.
